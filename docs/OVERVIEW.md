# Smart Attend — Overview

QR-and-GPS verified attendance for modern classrooms.

---

## The Problem

- Paper sign-in sheets get lost, forged, or filled in by a friend.
- Lecturers waste five to ten minutes per class calling names.
- No reliable record for exams, funding, or accreditation.
- Students who skip class leave no trace.

---

## The Solution

- Lecturers display a rotating QR code. It refreshes every twenty seconds.
- Students scan with their phones. No app installation needed.
- GPS verifies the student is physically inside the classroom radius.
- Attendance is captured instantly, timestamped, and location-verified.

---

## Key Features

- **60-second onboarding** per student.
- **Anti-proxy protection** with server-side distance verification.
- **Live attendance views** that update every five seconds.
- **Automatic email warnings** for at-risk students.
- **PDF exports** with institution letterhead, signatures, and stamp area.
- **Fully brandable** with a custom logo, colours, favicon, and footer.
- **One-click backups** with restore.
- **Dark mode** and responsive design.

---

## Roles and Capabilities

**Student**

- View enrolled courses and current attendance percentage.
- Scan QR to mark attendance.
- Download a personal attendance record as a PDF.
- Update profile photo.

**Lecturer**

- Start sessions in one click with GPS-captured anchor.
- Display a live rotating QR code.
- View live attendance in real time.
- Manage own course enrollment.
- Download register PDFs.

**Administrator**

- Manage users, courses, and enrollments.
- Bulk-enroll students via CSV upload.
- Filter and export reports.
- Notify at-risk students by email.
- Brand the system.
- Back up and restore the database.
