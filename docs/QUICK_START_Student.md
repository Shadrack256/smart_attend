# Smart Attend — Quick Start for Students

Your attendance, one scan away.

---

## Step 1 — Log In

1. Open the URL your lecturer shares.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. You land on your dashboard.

Forgot your password? On the login page, click **Forgot password?** and follow the instructions.

---

## Step 2 — Mark Attendance

When your lecturer starts a session:

1. Tap the big **Scan QR** button on your dashboard.
2. Allow camera and location when your browser asks.
3. Point your phone at the QR code on the lecturer's screen.
4. Hold steady for one to two seconds.
5. You will see: **Attendance marked as present**.

The confirmation also shows your distance from the classroom centre.

---

## Step 3 — Check Your Percentage

Your dashboard lists each of your courses with an attendance bar.

- Green — 75% or higher. Safe.
- Amber — 50 to 74%. Warning.
- Red — below 50%. At risk.

Keep an eye on this. Many institutions require a minimum of 75% to sit exams.

---

## Step 4 — Download Your Record

1. On any course card, click **Download my report**.
2. A PDF opens with every session you attended, timestamped and verified.

Keep a copy for your own records.

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

**You are X metres from the class**

You are too far from the lecturer. Move closer and try again.

**QR expired. Scan again.**

The QR rotates every twenty seconds. Simply point your camera at the code again.

**Attendance already marked**

You have already scanned for this session. No action needed.

**You are not enrolled in this course**

Contact your administrator. You need to be enrolled before you can mark attendance.

**Camera will not open**

The URL must start with `https://`. If it does, check your browser's camera permission for this site.

**Location is not detected**

Enable location services in your phone's settings and allow location in the browser for this URL.

---

## Changing Your Photo

1. Click your name or avatar at the top of the dashboard.
2. Click the upload area or drag a photo into it.
3. Choose a JPG, PNG, or WebP under 2 MB.
4. Click **Save photo**.

---

## Need Help?

Contact your lecturer or your administrator. For technical issues, include the exact error message and a screenshot if possible.
