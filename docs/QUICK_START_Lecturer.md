# Smart Attend — Quick Start for Lecturers

Start a session in three clicks.

---

## Step 1 — Log In

1. Open the URL provided by your institution.
2. Enter your staff email and password.
3. You land on your dashboard showing your courses.

---

## Step 2 — Start a Session

1. Click the **Start session** button.
2. Choose the course.
3. Set the geofence radius. Recommended: **100 metres**. Use **300 metres** for indoor classrooms with poor GPS.
4. Allow location when your browser asks.
5. Click **Start session**.

You land on the live QR display.

> **Tip:** Use a phone to start sessions, not a laptop. Phone GPS is accurate to about five metres. Laptop GPS can be off by hundreds of metres, which causes students to be wrongly rejected.

---

## Step 3 — Display the QR

- The QR code appears on screen and refreshes every twenty seconds.
- Students scan it from their phones.
- A countdown shows the time until the next refresh.

A screenshot of the QR becomes useless within twenty seconds. That is the first line of defence against proxy attendance.

---

## Step 4 — End the Session

1. Click **End session** when class is done.
2. Confirm the dialog.
3. Every enrolled student who did not scan is automatically marked **absent**.

Sessions also close automatically after three hours if you forget.

---

## Extra Tools

### Live attendance view

Click **View live attendance** on the QR page to see the full roster, colour-coded by status. It refreshes every five seconds.

- **Present** — scanned within ten minutes of start.
- **Late** — scanned ten minutes or more after start.
- **Waiting** — not yet scanned.
- **Absent** — set when the session ends.

### Download a register PDF

On your dashboard, click **Register** on any course. You get a PDF with one row per student and one column per session, with a tick for present, L for late, and a cross for absent.

The PDF carries the institution letterhead, signatures, and stamp area.

### Enroll students (when admin is away)

Click **Manage students** on any of your courses:

- **Enroll** new students by ticking the checkboxes and clicking **Enroll**.
- **Remove** students with the Remove button next to any name.
- **Search** the enrolled roster.

You cannot remove a student who already has attendance records for the course. That protects historical data.

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

**Students say "I'm too far"**

Restart the session with a larger radius, for example 500 metres. Or start the session from a phone for a more accurate anchor.

**QR does not appear**

Refresh the page. If the problem continues, contact your administrator.

**End session button missing**

It only appears on the live QR page during an active session.

**Session stuck open for hours**

It will close automatically after three hours. You can also click **End session** if you still have the QR page open.
