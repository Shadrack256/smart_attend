How it works

Three simple steps

From the lecturer's first click to the student's confirmed attendance — in seconds.

01

Lecturer starts the session

One click on the dashboard records time and location, and generates a live QR code.

02

Students scan the code

The camera reads the QR, phone location is captured, and the server verifies everything.

03

Attendance is auto-captured

The record is saved with status, timestamp, and distance. Reports update instantly.
